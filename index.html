USEF Pony Finals — The Pinnacle of Pony Competition

The USEF Pony Hunter National Championship, presented by Marshall + Sterling, is held annually at the Kentucky Horse Park in Lexington, Kentucky each August — the most prestigious pony competition in the country. Typically more than 600 ponies compete each year across six divisions: small green, medium green, large green, small regular, medium regular, and large regular. Each division is judged in three phases: the model (conformation and way of moving), under saddle, and over fences — with the model counting 25%, under saddle 25%, and over fences 50% of the final score.

To qualify for Pony Finals, a pony must earn a Champion or Reserve Champion at a USEF Premier ("AA") or National ("A") rated show during the qualifying period. Ponies must be registered with both USEF and USHJA and have a valid current measurement card. A Pony Finals-qualified pony represents the gold standard in the pony hunter market and is among the most sought-after ponies for competitive junior families.

Green Pony Hunters for Sale

The USEF Pony Finals green divisions are for ponies in their first or second year competing at the pony hunter height. A green pony can only compete in the green section at Pony Finals once — making green Pony Finals participation a unique milestone in a pony's career. Green pony hunters are ideal for programs looking to develop a pony for the long term, or for families who want a talented young pony with the potential to grow into a competitive regular division pony. Green ponies qualify for Pony Finals by winning one Championship or two Reserve Championships in a USEF Premier or National rated green pony section during the qualifying period.

Short stirrup ponies must be genuinely safe, patient, and forgiving. The ideal short stirrup pony is calm in the ring, consistent over small fences, and kind with young and inexperienced riders. Leadline Ponies for Sale

Leadline ponies carry the very youngest riders — typically toddlers and children under 6 — in the leadline division at horse shows. The ideal leadline pony is exceptionally quiet, steady, and gentle with small children. They need to be completely bombproof, comfortable with distractions, and happy being led around the ring. Pony Jumper Prospects for Sale

The USEF Pony Jumper National Championships are held each year at Pony Finals at the Kentucky Horse Park, featuring small (.60–.70m, riders 12 & under), medium (.70–.80m, riders 14 & under), and large (.80–.90m, riders 16 & under) categories. Pony jumper prospects must be athletic, careful, and scopey — qualities that make them exciting to develop for the national championship level. Marshall + Sterling / USEF Pony Medal

The Marshall + Sterling / USEF Pony Medal Final is held at USEF Pony Finals each year in the Alltech Arena at the Kentucky Horse Park. Riders must earn 30 qualifying points in USEF Pony Medal classes during the qualifying period to be eligible for the Final. Pony Medal horses must have the movement and adjustability for equitation-style rounds — an additional quality that makes a pony medal-eligible mount one of the most versatile and valued ponies on the market.
